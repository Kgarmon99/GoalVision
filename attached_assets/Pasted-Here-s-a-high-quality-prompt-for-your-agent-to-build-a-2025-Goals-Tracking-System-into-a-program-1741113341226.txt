### **Prompt: Build a 2025 Goals Execution & Progress Tracking Program**  

#### **Objective:**  
Create a **highly visual, automated, and interactive** system to track execution and progress for 2025 goals. This should include a **dashboard**, **weekly execution tracker**, and **progress tracking system** to measure funding, revenue, user growth, and school expansion goals.  

---

### **Core Features & Requirements:**  

#### **1️⃣ Dashboard Overview (Real-Time Visualization)**  
- **Dynamic Progress Bars & KPIs:**  
  - Funding progress (target: **$10M**)  
  - Revenue progress (target: **$100M**)  
  - User growth (target: **100M active users**)  
  - School expansion (target: **10,000 schools**)  
- **Metrics Display:**  
  - **MRR & ARR** (Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue)  
  - **Churn Rate & Retention**  
  - **Daily/Weekly/Monthly Active Users (DAU/WAU/MAU)**  
  - **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**  
  - **School onboarding rate** (e.g., **1,000 schools/month**)  

---

#### **2️⃣ Weekly Execution Tracker (Automated Updates)**  
- **Auto-pull** weekly tasks from predefined execution roadmap  
- Track **completion status** (✅ Done, 🔄 In Progress, ❌ Missed)  
- Generate **weekly reports** with % of completion, blockers, and priorities  

---

#### **3️⃣ Progress Tracking System**  
- **Completion % Calculation** for each major goal  
- **Red-Yellow-Green Status Indicators**  
  - ✅ **Green** = On Track  
  - 🟡 **Yellow** = Needs Attention  
  - 🔴 **Red** = Off Track  
- **Historical trend analysis** with weekly/monthly snapshots  

---

#### **4️⃣ Smart Alerts & Automation**  
- Auto-reminders for upcoming deadlines  
- Slack/email notifications for overdue milestones  
- Auto-adjust **goal pacing** based on real-time data  

---

#### **5️⃣ Data Input & Management**  
- **Manual & Automated Data Entry:**  
  - Allow manual updates for tracking meetings, investor deals, and user growth  
  - Integrate with Google Sheets, Notion, or Airtable for automatic updates  
- **APIs & Integrations:**  
  - Plug into Zapier, Stripe (for revenue tracking), and Google Analytics (for user growth)  

---

### **Tech Stack (Recommended)**  
- **Frontend:** React.js or Next.js for interactive UI  
- **Backend:** Node.js or Python (FastAPI/Django) for data processing  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL or Firebase for structured tracking  
- **Visualization:** Plotly, D3.js, or Chart.js for graphs  
- **Automation:** Zapier, Google Sheets API, or Notion API  

---

### **Key Deliverables**  
1. **Interactive dashboard** with goal progress tracking  
2. **Automated weekly execution tracker** with completion statuses  
3. **Live metrics dashboard** displaying funding, revenue, and user growth  
4. **Smart alerts & reminders** for milestones and pacing adjustments  

---

### **Final Notes:**  
This system should be **visually engaging, intuitive, and gamified** to maximize motivation. It must be **fast, responsive, and scalable** to handle high growth. 🚀
