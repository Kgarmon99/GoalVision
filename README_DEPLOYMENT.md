## ⚡ Quick Start (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Import to Replit
1. Go to [replit.com](https://replit.com) and sign up (free!)
2. Click "Create Repl" → "Import from GitHub"
3. Paste your repository URL
4. Click "Import"

### Step 2: Run It!
1. Click the **"Run"** button
2. Wait 1-2 minutes
3. **Done!** Your app is live!

### Step 3: Get Your URL
Look at the top of Replit - you'll see your public URL:
```
https://your-app-name.your-username.repl.co
```

**Open it on your phone, computer, anywhere!** 📱💻

## 🎯 Data Storage Options

### Option 1: In-Memory (Easiest)
- No configuration needed
- Just click "Run"
- Data resets on restart (perfect for testing)

### Option 2: Replit's Database (Persistent)
1. Click "Secrets" tab (🔒 icon)
2. Add: `USE_DATABASE` = `true`
3. Replit provides the database automatically!
4. Restart your app

## 📱 Mobile Access

1. Copy your Replit URL
2. Open in your phone's browser
3. **It works!** No app needed

**Tip:** Add to home screen for quick access!

## 🔄 Keeping It Running

**Free accounts:**

- Or click "Run" to wake it up
