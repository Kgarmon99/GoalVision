# 🚀 Simple Deployment - 3 Steps

Your app is ready to deploy! Here's the easiest way:

## Option 1: Railway (Easiest - 2 minutes)

1. Go to: https://railway.app
2. Click "Start a New Project"
3. Click "Deploy from GitHub repo"
4. Select your **GoalVision** repo
5. **Done!** Railway does everything automatically.

That's it! You'll get a URL like: `https://goalvision.up.railway.app`

---

## Option 2: Fly.io (Also Easy)

1. Install Fly CLI: `iwr https://fly.io/install.ps1 -useb | iex` (in PowerShell)
2. Run: `fly launch` (in your project folder)
3. Follow the prompts
4. Done!
